How to play

Fill squares so every row and column matches its numbers. Each number is a run of filled squares, in order, with at least one empty square between runs. Finish and a picture appears.

  • 3 1 3 1 in five squares fits one way: three, a gap, one.
  • 4 A 4 in five squares sits in one of two places (the bars). The middle three are filled either way.
  • 3 Hold a square (or right-click it) to mark it empty with a dot, once you know it can't be filled.
  • 5 Hold a row's or column's numbers (or right-click them) to lock a finished line, so stray taps can't change it. Hold again to unlock.
  • Tap a square to fill it; tap again to clear it.
  • Hold or right-click to mark a square empty with a dot. Do it again to clear the dot.
  • A number fades once its run is finished and closed off by a dot or the edge. That's your bookkeeping, not a check: only the finished picture says you're right.
  • Drag to fill a line, or a block: drag across and down.
  • Arrows move · Space fill · X mark · Z undo
  • L locks or unlocks the focused square’s row; Shift+L its column.
  • Bottom left: undo, start over, and mark mode, which makes taps place dots instead of filling. Tap the level number to pick a level.
  • Settings, bottom right, has Quick play (a fresh random board at the size you choose), Zoom, the light or dark theme, and Reset. Hold the empty background to open it from anywhere. After a Quick play board, Share blank sends that same unsolved board to a friend.
