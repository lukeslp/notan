Notan Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

Data collection

Notan does not ask for an account and does not collect personal information. The iPhone, iPad and Android apps contain no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Network use

The apps make no network requests. The game, its fonts and its pictures are bundled inside the app, so it works with no connection and sends nothing anywhere. The Android app does not even hold the internet permission. A link you tap yourself opens in your browser and leaves the app.

The website at playnotan.com counts page views with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and stores no personal information.

On-device data

Solved levels, best times, boards in progress and your settings are stored on your device so you can pick up where you left off. They are never sent to the developer. Deleting the app removes them.

Children

Notan collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
